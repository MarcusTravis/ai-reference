Common questions

Which AI video generators had the most website visits in this chart?

Among rows with a cited visit figure, Kling had 16.1 million web visits in October 2026, HeyGen 9.7 million, Runway 7.5 million, Vidu 4.0 million, Luma Dream Machine 2.25 million, and Pika 1.06 million (Similarweb estimates via SeekTool). Hailuo had 5.9 million visits in September 2026 (Semrush estimates via Tako’s a16z Top 100 table). Google Veo, Sora, and Adobe Firefly Video popularity scores are editorial estimates, because a clean product-level usage figure was not used for those rows.

What do Runway, Sora, and Veo typical plans cost?

Runway’s typical plan in this chart is Standard at $15 a month, or $12 a month billed annually (runwayml.com/pricing, checked November 2026). Sora access is rated on ChatGPT Plus at $20 a month, and Google Veo access is rated on Google AI Pro at $19.99 a month. Kling, Luma Dream Machine, Pika, Hailuo, Adobe Firefly Video, HeyGen, and Vidu have editorial cost bands, because no public individual price was captured this session. No dollar amounts were invented for those rows.

Are the popularity scores a single industry ranking?

No. Scores are relative to the other video tools on this page. Public, product-level usage rankings for video generators are thin, and some well-known tools (Sora, Veo) are labeled as editorial estimates for popularity.

Does this chart include talking-head or avatar video tools?

HeyGen is on this chart. The other rows are mainly text-to-video or image-to-video tools such as Runway, Kling, Luma Dream Machine, Pika, Sora, Google Veo, Hailuo, Vidu, and Adobe Firefly Video.

How the ratings work

  • Popularity is a 1–5 judgment (halves allowed) of how commonly a product shows up in recent traffic reports, app ranks, or usage leaderboards — relative to the other products on this page, not a computed formula.
  • Captions name the source and a “checked Nov 2026” date. If there was no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”
  • Best for is an editorial label of a typical use, not a test score.
  • Cost is a five-step scale (Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$). See “How cost is rated” for the band definitions on this page. Captions cite a source or say the rating is an estimate.
  • Scores are relative to the other video tools on this page.

How cost is rated

  • Cost is rated on the typical individual paid plan most people buy, per month, when we could find a published price this session. If the vendor marks a plan as popular or most popular, that is the plan we use. Otherwise we use the ordinary individual paid plan (not the cheapest limited tier, and not an ultra or teams tier). The caption names the plan. Annual “per month” equivalents are noted when the vendor publishes them. Higher ultra tiers do not set the band.
  • Free — no individual paid plan is required for the core product.
  • $ — under $15 / month.
  • $$ — $15–29 / month.
  • $$$ — $30–99 / month.
  • $$$$ — $100+ / month, or quote-only with no public individual plan found this session.
  • If the product is mainly a usage-based API, or no public individual price turned up, the caption says so and the band is an editorial estimate — never an invented dollar amount. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor.

Sources and limits
