    Common questions

    What is the AI Reference Library?

    It is a free, ad-free set of comparison charts of 126 AI tools in 12 sections: chatbots, image generation, photo editing and enhancing, video, voice, music, coding assistants, transcription, AI search, agents and automation, business and everyday AI apps, and 3D generation. Each section is a compact chart you can filter, search, and sort. Official-site links go to the vendor and are not affiliate links.

    How are the popularity ratings sourced?

    Popularity is a 1–5 judgment (halves allowed) of how commonly a product shows up in recent traffic reports, app ranks, or usage leaderboards, relative to the other products on the same page — not a computed formula. Captions name the source and a checked date. This pass used Similarweb website-visit estimates for October 2026 (often as published by SeekTool), some Semrush estimates, GitHub star counts, OpenRouter token rankings, and app-store rating counts. Website-visit figures are visits, not app sessions or unique people. If there was no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.” Product data was last checked November 15, 2026.

    Is the site free, and are there ads or affiliate links?

    Yes. The site is free and ad-free, and it has no affiliate links. Official-site buttons go straight to the vendor. Stars are not paid placement. If the charts help you, the footer has an optional Ko-fi link.

    How is cost rated?

    Cost uses a five-step scale: Free, $, $$, $$$, and $$$$. It is rated on the typical individual paid plan most people buy, per month, when a published price was found: the plan the vendor marks as popular or most popular when they mark one, otherwise the ordinary individual paid plan (not the cheapest limited tier, and not an ultra or teams tier). The bands are: Free — no individual paid plan is required for the core product; $ — under $15 a month; $$ — $15–29 a month; $$$ — $30–99 a month; $$$$ — $100 or more a month, or quote-only with no public individual plan found. If the product is mainly a usage-based API, or no public individual price turned up, the caption says so and the band is an editorial estimate — never an invented dollar amount. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor. Prices were checked in November 2026.

    How to use this site

    Search above to jump straight to any product’s row, or open a section. Each section opens as a compact, scrollable comparison chart — one row per product — that you can filter, search, and sort. Stars are a 1–5 popularity judgment with a source caption. Cost uses a Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$ scale with a source caption, rated on the typical individual paid plan most people buy. “Best for” labels are editorial. Official-site links are not affiliate links.

    Where the numbers come from

    Web-traffic figures are third-party estimates of website visits, not app sessions or unique people. Chatbot shares among seven assistants (October 2026) come from Similarweb: ChatGPT 55.7%, Gemini 24.2%, Claude 11.5%, DeepSeek 3.4%, Grok 2.2%, Copilot 1.1%, Perplexity 0.8%. Most other visit counts are Similarweb estimates for October 2026, as published by SeekTool. A few use Semrush estimates for September 2026 from Tako’s a16z Top 100 table, GitHub star counts, or OpenRouter token rankings. Cost figures come from official vendor pricing pages, checked in November 2026, and are rated on the typical individual paid plan most people buy. If a product has no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”

    What this is not

    This is not a vendor ranking you should buy from blindly. It is a starting map for people learning the AI tools landscape. Model versions change quickly; confirm current pricing and terms with the vendor.
