    How to use this site

    Search above to jump straight to any product’s row, or open a section. Each section opens as a compact, scrollable comparison chart — one row per product — that you can filter, search, and sort. Stars are a 1–5 popularity judgment with a source caption. Cost uses a Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$ scale with a source caption, rated on a typical individual monthly plan. “Best for” labels are editorial. Official-site links are not affiliate links.

    Where the numbers come from

    The main web-traffic source for chatbots is Similarweb’s estimates of visits to generative AI assistant websites (October 2026 shares: ChatGPT 55.7%, Gemini 24.2%, Claude 11.5%, DeepSeek 3.4%, Grok 2.2%, Copilot 1.1%, Perplexity 0.8%). Those figures count website visits, not app or in-product use. API-side usage is cited from OpenRouter’s public model rankings (tokens processed, week of mid-November 2026) where that is the more honest signal. Cost figures come from official vendor pricing pages, checked in November 2026. If a product has no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”

    What this is not

    This is not a vendor ranking you should buy from blindly. It is a starting map for people learning the AI tools landscape. Model versions change quickly; confirm current pricing and terms with the vendor.
