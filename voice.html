Common questions

Which voice or text-to-speech tool had the most website visits here?

ElevenLabs had 42.4 million web visits in October 2026 (Similarweb estimates via SeekTool), the highest cited visit count on this page. Speechify had 5.46 million, Descript 3.27 million, and Cartesia 1.78 million on the same source. OpenAI TTS, PlayAI, Murf, and Google Cloud TTS popularity scores are editorial estimates.

What does ElevenLabs’s typical plan cost?

ElevenLabs’s typical plan in this chart is Creator at $22 a month, or $11 for the first month, labeled popular (elevenlabs.io/pricing, checked November 2026). Starter is $6 a month after a $1 first month. OpenAI TTS is rated on ChatGPT Plus at $20 a month for ChatGPT voice features; API TTS is usage-based and billed separately. Most other voice rows have editorial cost bands, because no flat public individual price was captured, or official pricing is usage-based.

Does this page cover meeting transcription?

Only in a limited way. Descript appears here as a voice and editing tool, and it also appears on the Transcription and speech-to-text chart. Meeting notetakers such as Otter, Fireflies, and Fathom are on that transcription chart.

Is music generation on this page?

No. Song and score generators such as Suno and Udio, and Eleven Music, are on the Music generation chart. Eleven Music is sold as part of ElevenLabs paid plans, but it is listed there rather than here.

How the ratings work

  • Popularity is a 1–5 judgment (halves allowed) of how commonly a product shows up in recent traffic reports, app ranks, or usage leaderboards — relative to the other products on this page, not a computed formula.
  • Captions name the source and a “checked Nov 2026” date. If there was no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”
  • Best for is an editorial label of a typical use, not a test score.
  • Cost is a five-step scale (Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$). See “How cost is rated” for the band definitions on this page. Captions cite a source or say the rating is an estimate.
  • Scores are relative to the other voice tools on this page.

How cost is rated

  • Cost is rated on the typical individual paid plan most people buy, per month, when we could find a published price this session. If the vendor marks a plan as popular or most popular, that is the plan we use. Otherwise we use the ordinary individual paid plan (not the cheapest limited tier, and not an ultra or teams tier). The caption names the plan. Annual “per month” equivalents are noted when the vendor publishes them. Higher ultra tiers do not set the band.
  • Free — no individual paid plan is required for the core product.
  • $ — under $15 / month.
  • $$ — $15–29 / month.
  • $$$ — $30–99 / month.
  • $$$$ — $100+ / month, or quote-only with no public individual plan found this session.
  • If the product is mainly a usage-based API, or no public individual price turned up, the caption says so and the band is an editorial estimate — never an invented dollar amount. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor.

Sources and limits

Voice tools mix consumer apps and developer APIs. Usage-based API pricing is not forced into a fake monthly total. Popularity is editorial unless a caption cites a source.
