Common questions

Which transcription tools had the most cited use in this chart?

OpenAI Whisper is rated on the open-source repo, with 112,711 GitHub stars (openai/whisper), checked in November 2026. Among meeting and service sites, Otter had 7.36 million web visits in October 2026, Fireflies 4.41 million, Descript 3.27 million, Notta 2.59 million, and Rev 1.52 million (Similarweb estimates via SeekTool).

What do Otter and Whisper cost in this chart?

Otter’s typical plan is Pro at $16.99 per user per month, or $8.33 per user per month billed annually (otter.ai/pricing, checked November 2026). The open-source Whisper models are free to run locally; hosted API use is billed separately. Most other rows have editorial cost bands, because many are usage-based APIs or no public individual price was captured this session.

Does this page mix APIs and meeting notetakers?

Yes. It includes developer speech-to-text APIs and meeting apps with monthly seats. Scores are relative to the other transcription tools on this page, and API cost bands are editorial estimates when there is no fair individual monthly plan. Whisper’s star count is for the open model, which is not the same as ChatGPT’s built-in voice features.

Where is Descript listed?

Descript appears on this transcription chart and also on the Voice, TTS, and cloning chart, because it is used for both editing voice and turning speech into text. The October 2026 visit figure cited on both rows is 3.27 million for the Descript site.

How the ratings work

  • Popularity is a 1–5 judgment (halves allowed) of how commonly a product shows up in recent traffic reports, app ranks, or usage leaderboards — relative to the other products on this page, not a computed formula.
  • Captions name the source and a “checked Nov 2026” date. If there was no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”
  • Best for is an editorial label of a typical use, not a test score.
  • Cost is a five-step scale (Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$). See “How cost is rated” for the band definitions on this page. Captions cite a source or say the rating is an estimate.
  • Scores are relative to the other transcription tools on this page.

How cost is rated

  • Cost is rated on the typical individual paid plan most people buy, per month, when we could find a published price this session. If the vendor marks a plan as popular or most popular, that is the plan we use. Otherwise we use the ordinary individual paid plan (not the cheapest limited tier, and not an ultra or teams tier). The caption names the plan. Annual “per month” equivalents are noted when the vendor publishes them. Higher ultra tiers do not set the band.
  • Free — no individual paid plan is required for the core product.
  • $ — under $15 / month.
  • $$ — $15–29 / month.
  • $$$ — $30–99 / month.
  • $$$$ — $100+ / month, or quote-only with no public individual plan found this session.
  • If the product is mainly a usage-based API, or no public individual price turned up, the caption says so and the band is an editorial estimate — never an invented dollar amount. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor.
