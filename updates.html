Site launch

Nov 14, 2026

The first version of the AI Reference Library: a homepage with search across every product, plus ten comparison charts — LLMs and chatbots, image generation, video generation, voice/TTS and cloning, music generation, coding assistants, transcription and speech-to-text (including meeting notetakers), AI search, agents and automation, and 3D generation. Each chart has filter chips, search, sort (including cheapest-first and priciest-first), a sticky header, and a sticky horizontal scrollbar. Popularity and cost captions were researched in November 2026. See CHANGELOG.md in the repository for the source list.
