How the ratings work

  • Popularity is a 1–5 judgment (halves allowed) of how commonly a product shows up in recent traffic reports, app ranks, or usage leaderboards — relative to the other products on this page, not a computed formula.
  • Captions name the source and a “checked Nov 2026” date. If there was no usable source, the caption says “Editorial estimate.”
  • Best for is an editorial label of a typical use, not a test score.
  • Cost is a five-step scale (Free / $ / $$ / $$$ / $$$$). See “How cost is rated” for the band definitions on this page. Captions cite a source or say the rating is an estimate.
  • Scores are relative to the other 3D tools on this page.

How cost is rated

  • Cost is rated on the typical individual paid plan most people buy, per month, when we could find a published price this session. If the vendor marks a plan as popular or most popular, that is the plan we use. Otherwise we use the ordinary individual paid plan (not the cheapest limited tier, and not an ultra or teams tier). The caption names the plan. Annual “per month” equivalents are noted when the vendor publishes them. Higher ultra tiers do not set the band.
  • Free — no individual paid plan is required for the core product.
  • $ — under $15 / month.
  • $$ — $15–29 / month.
  • $$$ — $30–99 / month.
  • $$$$ — $100+ / month, or quote-only with no public individual plan found this session.
  • If the product is mainly a usage-based API, or no public individual price turned up, the caption says so and the band is an editorial estimate — never an invented dollar amount. Always confirm current pricing with the vendor.

Sources and limits

The 3D generation field is smaller and faster-moving than chat or image tools. Most popularity scores on this page are editorial estimates. Official pricing is cited when a public individual plan was found.
